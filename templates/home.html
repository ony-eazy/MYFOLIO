Why we built this !!!

Myfolio is a place where anyone can build a clean portfolio and show the world what they make. Whether you write code, design, take photos, or create anything else, this is where your work gets a home instead of being scattered across a dozen apps.

I built this because I kept seeing talented people struggle to present themselves well. Their work was good, but it lived in random folders, old links, and posts nobody could find. As a Computer Science student, I wanted one simple, good-looking spot where your portfolio speaks for you — no clutter, no design skills required.

It's still a work in progress, and I'm building it in the open. Every time I learn something new I come back and make it better.

How it works

Make your profile, add the work you're proud of, and share one clean link. No templates to wrestle with — you focus on the work, and Showcase makes it look like it belongs.
